What the strip does

  • Lives in the Infrastructure architecture panel, so the run and the diagram are on one screen.
  • Step ticks share one time base (seconds from Play); steps at the same second merge into one tick (1·2 ✓).
  • The step under the playhead outlines its parts (solid ring) and the declared blast radius (red dashes).
  • Earlier steps keep a faint ring; later ones nothing.
  • Arrow keys move one step (WAI-ARIA slider); the caption is the text, the ring only helps.

After the run the same strip replays the recorded steps. It does not replay past readings: node colours stay live, and the strip says so.
